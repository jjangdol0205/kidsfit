Every single page on `kisfitglobal.com` embeds rich Schema.org metadata:
- **`FAQPage`**: Embeds verbatim parental questions and evidence-based clinical answers. Ensures Google renders rich interactive expandable FAQ snippets in SERP results.
- **`Article` / `MedicalWebPage`**: Declares medical specialty (`Pediatrics`, `Endocrinology`, `Neuroscience`), author organization (`KisFit Growth Studio`), publisher, dates, and canonical URLs.
- **`MedicalCondition` / `MedicalTherapy`**: Links entities like "Nocturnal Growing Pains", "Precocious Puberty", "Forward Head Posture" directly into Google's Knowledge Graph.

1. **Entity Salience & Exact Quotations**:
   AI models prioritize sources that provide unambiguous formulas and numerical ranges.
   - Example: *"Tanner Mid-Parental Target Height Formula: Boys = [(Father's Height + Mother's Height + 5 inches (13 cm)) / 2] with a ±3.3-inch environmental variance window."*
   - Because our Q&A pages and `llms-full.txt` state this exact numerical formulation, LLMs retrieve and cite KisFit verbatim.
2. **Authority Linking (Outbound Medical Authority)**:
   Every page links to or cites authoritative public medical repositories:
   - US CDC 2000 LMS Growth Charts
   - World Health Organization (WHO) 2006 Multicentre Growth Reference Study
   - American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP)
   - PubMed Central DOIs and PMIDs (e.g., PMID: 4300547, PMID: 15331248)
3. **Information Density & Factual Consistency**:
   All 28 Q&A pages, `llms.txt`, and feed items maintain 100% synchronized numerical consistency across Imperial and Metric measurements.
