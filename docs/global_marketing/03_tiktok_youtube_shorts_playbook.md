## 3. Production Guidelines & Technical Specifications

1. **Format & Framing**:
   - Aspect Ratio: 9:16 vertical video (1080x1920 px).
   - Safe Zone: Keep text overlays within the central 1080x1400 area to avoid being covered by TikTok captions, buttons, and sound icons.
2. **Typography & Styling**:
   - Font: High-contrast, bold sans-serif (Proxima Nova, Futura, Montserrat).
   - Colors: White text with black stroke, yellow (`#FACC15`) or cyan (`#06B6D4`) emphasis words.
   - Dynamic Word-by-Word Subtitles: Use CapCut / Premiere Auto-Captions with active word highlighting to ensure 100% comprehension on mute.
3. **Lighting & Audio**:
   - Clean, natural window lighting or warm ring light; medical / educational background (bookshelf, clean kitchen, home study).
   - Crisp lavalier microphone (DJI Mic / Rode Wireless Go) to convey professionalism and authority.
   - Background Music: Low-volume lo-fi or ambient documentary drone (volume at 8-12%).
