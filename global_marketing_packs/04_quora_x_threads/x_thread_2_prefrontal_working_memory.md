### Tweet 2 (The 4-Chunk Limit):
1/ The human prefrontal cortex holds a maximum of 4 ± 1 information chunks in working memory at once.

In simple calculations (48 - 19), all 4 slots are dedicated to computation.

In a word problem, the child has to decode text, suppress story fluff, track narrative time, and remember numbers all at once.

Their mental buffer overflows before they can even begin calculating.

---

### Tweet 3 (The Feeling of Freezing):
2/ When the working memory buffer hits 100% capacity:

The brain triggers an acute stress response. Cortisol spikes, the child freezes, guesses blindly, or bursts into tears.

Drilling more rote math flashcards or reading comprehension worksheets only increases the cognitive load and worsens the anxiety.

---

### Tweet 4 (The 10-Second Margin Fix):
3/ The solution is to externally offload working memory onto paper.

We use the 10-Second Margin Bar Modeling technique:
• Blue Box around given quantities.
• Red Circle around the final question target.
• Draw a rectangular box in the margin with Parts and Total.

---

### Tweet 5 (Spatial Anchoring):
4/ Why does drawing a simple margin box work?

It converts abstract semantic relationships into visual-spatial coordinates processed by the right parietal lobe.

This immediately frees up 60% of prefrontal working memory capacity, allowing the child to compute the arithmetic effortlessly.

---

### Tweet 6 (The Dopamine Screen Factor):
5/ High screen time compounds working memory deficits.

15-second algorithmic video loops (TikTok, Shorts) flood the striatum with supra-physiological dopamine, raising the baseline threshold.

Slower text-based tasks feel physically painful to an overstimulated brain.

---

### Tweet 7 (The 14-Day Reset Protocol):
6/ The 14-Day Executive Reset:

1. Turn tablet displays to grayscale mode (eliminates visual dopamine triggers).
2. Out-of-bedroom charging dock 60 minutes before bed.
3. 20-minute uninterrupted study intervals using an analog visual timer.
4. Replace passive screen time with tactile builds (Lego, woodworking, swimming).
