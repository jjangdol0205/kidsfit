# Quora Answer #5: What is the best calcium supplement for growing kids, and why does carbonate cause constipation?

## Answer Body:

**Short Answer:**  
The most clinically effective form of calcium for growing children is **Calcium Citrate** or **Microcrystalline Hydroxyapatite**, paired in a synergistic ratio with **Vitamin D3** and **Vitamin K2 (MK-7)**. 

Standard **Calcium Carbonate** (found in most cheap chewables) requires substantial stomach acid to ionize; because children have lower gastric acid secretion than adults, carbonate passes unabsorbed into the colon, binding with fatty acids to form hard insoluble soaps that cause severe pediatric constipation, gas, and stomach cramps.

---

### Comparison: Calcium Carbonate vs. Calcium Citrate

| Metric | Calcium Carbonate | Calcium Citrate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Acid Dependency** | Requires heavy hydrochloric acid (HCl) | Dissolves independently of gastric pH |
| **Bioavailability** | ~20% to 24% absorption | 2.5x higher bioavailability (~40%) |
| **Pediatric Side Effects** | Severe constipation, hard stools, bloating | Extremely gentle on digestion, zero constipation |
| **Timing Flexibility** | Must be taken immediately with a large meal | Can be taken on an empty stomach or between meals |

---

### The Synergistic "Bone Lock" Trio: Calcium + D3 + K2 (MK-7)
Taking calcium alone is inadequate and potentially counterproductive:
1. **Calcium Citrate**: Provides the raw elemental mineral building block.
2. **Vitamin D3 (1000-2000 IU)**: Stimulates intestinal synthesis of Calbindin proteins to transport calcium ions across the mucosal brush border into the bloodstream.
3. **Vitamin K2 MK-7 (45-75 mcg)**: Carboxylates the bone protein **Osteocalcin**. Without Vitamin K2, calcium remains circulating in the blood and deposits into kidneys or vascular tissue; K2 acts as the molecular key that locks calcium into skeletal trabecular bone.
