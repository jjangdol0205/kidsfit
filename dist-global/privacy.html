Privacy Policy

At KISFIT Bio Labs (https://kisfitglobal.com), protecting your family's privacy is foundational to our engineering. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how biometric calculations are processed locally on your device, and your rights under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Client-Side Biometric Calculation (Privacy by Design)

When a parent enters a child's height, weight, birth month/year, parental heights, or study habits into our 60-second assessment wizard, all growth percentile and Brain LQ calculations are executed directly inside your web browser and cached in your device's local storage (`localStorage`) so you can revisit and print your report. We do not require you to enter your child's full legal name, home address, or Social Security number.

2. Information Collected During Checkout & Account Verification

  • Parent Email Address & Order Metadata: When you purchase a report pass or register a parent member account, we collect your parent email address and Lemon Squeezy Order ID solely to verify your purchase, unlock your PRO clinical pass across devices, and deliver order receipts.
  • Payment Details: All credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal data are processed directly by our PCI-DSS Level 1 certified Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy, LLC. KISFIT Bio Labs never stores raw credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Anonymous Analytics: We record aggregated, non-identifiable page views and button interactions to improve clinical report readability and service reliability.

3. Children's Privacy (US COPPA Compliance)

KISFIT Bio Labs is designed exclusively for use by parents and legal guardians aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly market to or solicit personal data directly from children under the age of 13.

4. Data Sharing & Affiliate Disclosure

We never sell, rent, or trade parent email addresses or child biometric inputs to third-party data brokers or advertisers. Clinical reports may include optional reference links to third-party nutritional products (such as iHerb) that may earn a qualifying commission at zero additional cost to you.

5. Your Rights (Access, Deletion & Opt-Out)

You may clear your locally saved child profile at any time by clearing your browser storage, or request complete deletion of your parent account and order verification record by emailing support@kisfitglobal.com.
