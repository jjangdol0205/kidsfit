Almost every parent knows the feeling. You walk out of a brief annual checkup with a hasty percentile scribbled on a visit sheet, yet at home you notice classmates or soccer teammates pulling half a head taller since last spring. Later that night, turning off the hallway lamp, you catch yourself wondering how much growing time your child truly has left.

When you search online for answers, most of what appears is either noisy supplement marketing or raw reference tables that never explain what a number means for your own child. Two ten-year-olds born eight months apart do not share the same reference line, and a wrist X-ray taken last year no longer reflects where your child’s growth plates stand today.

We built KISFIT Studio to read your child’s growth clock calmly and honestly. Across five gentle questions, we compare your child’s exact age in days against public US CDC growth standards, weigh both parents’ heights, adjust any past wrist bone-age reading for the months that have passed since that visit (and if you have never had an X-ray, that is completely fine), and highlight the evening habits worth tending tonight.
